Dr. David Rogoff

General Dentist

Dr. David Rogoff earned his undergraduate degree in Biological Sciences with a minor in microbiology at the University of Maryland. He is a graduate of New York University College of Dentistry. Dr. Rogoff did his residency in general dentistry at Mount Sinai Hospital in Manhattan where he was trained in treatment of general dentistry, pediatric dentistry, endodontic, periodontal, prosthodontics and cosmetic dentistry. Dr. Rogoff grew up working in Rockaway at the family business of Rogoff’s Stationary Toy Store and at the bicycle shop. Dr. Rogoff’s main passion is the art of Sciences of Dentistry and the positive interaction with his patients.
