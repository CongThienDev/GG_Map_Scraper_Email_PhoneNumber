Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

A dental emergency includes severe toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, a dental abscess with significant swelling, a lost crown or filling causing pain, or oral bleeding that will not stop. If you are not sure whether your situation is urgent, call Beam Dental Union Square and we will help you figure it out.

Can I get a same-day dental appointment at Beam Dental Union Square?

Beam Dental Union Square does its best to accommodate same-day emergency visits. Call as early as possible - we open at 9am Monday, 9:30am Tuesday and Wednesday, and 8am Friday. Note that we are closed Thursdays and Sundays.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency near Union Square?

Call Beam Dental Union Square immediately. With direct subway access from the 4, 5, 6, L, N, Q, R, and W trains at 14th St-Union Sq, you can reach us quickly from almost anywhere in Manhattan, Brooklyn, or the outer boroughs. For a medical emergency or severe swelling affecting your airway, call 911 rather than waiting.

Does Beam Dental Union Square offer emergency dental appointments?

Yes. Beam Dental Union Square offers emergency dental care and will work to get you seen as quickly as possible. Our central Manhattan location is reachable via multiple subway lines, making it one of the most accessible emergency dentist options in New York City. Call our Union Square office right away and we will prioritize your case.

What is teeth whitening and how long do results last?

Professional teeth whitening at Beam Dental Union Square uses a clinically formulated treatment that lifts deep stains and brightens your smile far more effectively than anything available over the counter. Results typically last 6 to 12 months, depending on habits like coffee, tea, or tobacco. Periodic touch-up treatments help you maintain those results between full whitening sessions.

What are ceramic veneers and how are they different from composite bonding?

Ceramic veneers are thin, custom-crafted porcelain shells permanently bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They offer a highly natural look, excellent durability, and long-lasting stain resistance. Composite bonding uses tooth-colored resin applied directly to your tooth in a single chair-side visit - it is faster and more affordable, but may require touch-ups over time. Our Union Square cosmetic dentist can help you decide which approach makes the most sense for your smile goals and budget.

What is the difference between Invisalign and Spark clear aligners?

Both Invisalign and Spark are clear, removable aligner systems that straighten teeth without metal braces, and both are offered at Beam Dental Union Square. Invisalign is the most widely used clear aligner brand in the world. Spark is a newer system known for its exceptional clarity and smooth, comfortable trimlines. Our Union Square orthodontic team will evaluate your bite and teeth during a consultation and recommend the right system for your goals.

How much do dental implants cost at Beam Dental Union Square?

Dental implant pricing at Beam Dental Union Square is based on your individual case - the number of implants, the restoration type, and any preparatory procedures needed. Beam Dental's specialists have completed over 10,000 successful implant procedures and offer transparent pricing that is more competitive than many Manhattan dental practices. Contact our Union Square office to schedule a consultation and get a clear, itemized estimate.

Contact our Union Square team, let us know you want to make the switch, and we will handle the rest. Bring a valid photo ID, your dental insurance card if applicable, and a list of any current medications. If you have prior dental X-rays from another provider, bring those to your Union Square appointment to avoid duplicate imaging and make your first visit more efficient.
